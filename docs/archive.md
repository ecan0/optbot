# Archived academic project

OptBot is an archived CYBER 215 academic research project. Data collection is closed. The public site remains online as a read-only demonstration of the study instrument and privacy notices.

## Retained public record

- [Final paper](../publications/Data%20In%2C%20or%20Privacy%20Out_.pdf)
- [Aggregate quantitative results](../publications/optbot-quantitative-results-20260803.zip)
- Source code, public study materials, and documentation

The results package contains only aggregate findings. It does not contain response identifiers, participant-level records, raw submissions, written responses, credentials, Terraform state, or private infrastructure details.

## Retained private record

The final frozen research snapshot and recoverable backup remain in protected AWS storage. The response table retains point-in-time recovery and deletion protection. The final snapshot is retained separately from its routine 30-day curated-data lifecycle.

## Retired operations

- New submissions are rejected before Turnstile verification or data storage
- Analytics workgroups are disabled to prevent accidental query charges
- No Glue trigger or EventBridge schedule runs archival analytics

Do not reactivate collection, analytics, or deployment without a new approved research and privacy review. Follow the public-repository boundary before publishing any additional material.
